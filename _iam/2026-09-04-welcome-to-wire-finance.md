**Welcome to Wire Finance**

Wire Finance is a fictional professional and financial services organisation created as a practical environment for exploring modern identity, security, and cloud operations.

With 25 employees working across Finance, HR, Operations, Sales, and IT, the company operates from a single office while supporting remote workers. Its technology environment is built around Microsoft 365 and Azure, with Microsoft Entra ID providing the initial identity foundation, alongside Intune, Microsoft Defender, and Microsoft Sentinel for endpoint management, threat protection, monitoring, and security operations.

As Wire Finance grows, its environment will evolve to include on-premises Active Directory and hybrid identity, creating a realistic platform for exploring how organisations design, secure, monitor, and respond to threats across modern and traditional infrastructure.

Rather than being just another lab, Wire Finance represents a growing business where every technical decision has a purpose — and every security control has a story behind it.
